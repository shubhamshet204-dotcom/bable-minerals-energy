Long Steel — TMT, Angles, Channels, Beams, MS Pipes, and DI Pipes

Long steel covers TMT, angles, channels, beams, MS pipes, and DI pipes. The category includes reinforcement steel, structural sections, and pipes for different construction and engineering needs.

AnglesMild steel angles are hot-rolled L-sections with equal or unequal legs, available in IS 2062 E250 and E350 grades, with galvanised variants also available.

ChannelsMS channels are hot-rolled U-sections supplied in ISMC and allied profiles, in grades conforming to IS 2062, for load-bearing structural work and ordered by profile designation.

BeamsSteel beams are hot-rolled I- and H-sections — ISMB, NPB, UB, UC, and monorail profiles — used as primary load-carrying members.

MS PipesMS pipes are ERW round pipes and square and rectangular hollow sections made from HR strip, supplied black or galvanised.
